        Read a receipt

        Open the photo in Photos, press and hold the text, tap Select All then Copy — that is iPhone's own text recognition, and it is far better at receipts than anything this app could do. Paste it below and only the numbers get kept. The photo is never stored.

        Holding

        What you put in and what it is worth now. Kept completely separate from your spending money — nothing here touches your allowance.

        Give units and a price and the value works itself out. A ticker or coin id also lets Update prices fetch it. Otherwise just type what it is worth.

        New group

        Groups are the headings your categories sit under. The only thing that changes behaviour is whether a group counts as bills.

        Groups only organise your categories. To keep something out of the day-to-day figures, pick it under “Kept out of the day-to-day figures” in settings.
